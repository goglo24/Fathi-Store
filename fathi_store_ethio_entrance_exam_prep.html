Physics: Unit 1

Question 1 of 45 | EUEE 2012 EC

ID: PHY-12-001

If a thermodynamic system undergoes a process in which its internal energy remains constant, which of the following statements is true?

Explanation

According to the First Law of Thermodynamics, ΔU = Q - W. If the internal energy remains constant (ΔU = 0), then 0 = Q - W, which means Q = W. Therefore, the heat added to the system equals the work done by the system.
